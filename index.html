About YouTube links

A YouTube link can be watched here, but it doesn't give us the video file, so it can't be edited in your browser. Toolora doesn't download YouTube videos. If it's your video, download it from YouTube Studio and upload it in the Upload tab.

Auto highlights

Finds loud / high-energy moments (laughs, cheers, shouting, music drops) and fast motion, then makes clips around them. It runs on this device; it can't tell what's funny or what will go viral.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. No account, no watermark unless you add one.

    Is my video uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser; the file never leaves your device.

    Why not YouTube links?

    A link only lets us embed the player, not read the file. Toolora doesn't download YouTube videos; upload your own copy instead.

    Why does export take as long as the clip?

    Each clip is played and recorded in real time, so a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds. Keep this tab open while it runs.

    What format do I get?

    MP4 where your browser can record it (Safari, recent Chrome), otherwise WebM. The file extension matches the real format.

    Supported input: whatever your browser can play, usually MP4 (H.264/AAC) and WebM (VP8/VP9). MOV from iPhones works in Safari; HEVC and AVI often don't play in Chrome or Firefox.
